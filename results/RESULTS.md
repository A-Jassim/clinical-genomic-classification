**Class-imbalance reference:** predicting Alive for every test patient would achieve 85.9% accuracy, 0% sensitivity for Dead, and AUC 0.5. The chosen model does not outperform this trivial baseline on accuracy; its AUC and minority-class detection answer different questions. Accuracy alone is insufficient here.

The selected model is determined by training validation AUC, not by test ranking. Validation AUC is a tuning score. The single held-out split is not an external validation study, and the gene panel is exploratory.
